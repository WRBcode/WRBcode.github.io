12/2025 — Now

  • Achieved 35.04 dB PSNR / 0.914 SSIM on SKM-TEA 3D knee MRI, surpassing the E2E-VarNet3D baseline; validated cross-anatomy transfer on a private UCLA liver MRI dataset.
  • Extended the 2D DISCO framework (CVPR 2025), cutting training memory ~8× while preserving local inductive biases for anatomical structure. → NeurIPS 2026 submission.

  • Validated on super-resolution, dropout reconstruction, and restoration — up to +2.11 dB PSNR / +0.04 SSIM on 3D BraTS, dominating L2+Sobel baselines on 2D PICAI.
